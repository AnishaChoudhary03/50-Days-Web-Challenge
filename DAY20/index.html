About Synexus Core

Synexus Core is a student-led technical community dedicated to innovation, collaboration, leadership, and hands-on learning through workshops, hackathons, and real-world engineering projects.

Our Mission

To empower engineering students with practical technical knowledge, teamwork, innovation, and leadership through project-based learning and community activities.

Our Vision

To become one of the most active student technical communities that encourages creativity, entrepreneurship, research, and lifelong learning.

Our Values

Innovation, Integrity, Leadership, Collaboration, Technical Excellence, Creativity, and Continuous Learning.
